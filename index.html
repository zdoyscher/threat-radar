CONTEXT — THE WORK ITSELF, NOT JUST THE ANSWER

Every other threat on this board commoditizes the answer. Agents commoditize the doing. An agent is an AI pointed at a goal that can then plan the steps, open the tools, browse the web, write and run code, fill in the systems and come back with the task finished — the consultant's “I'll take care of it” turned into software. This pass is a useful reminder that the agent story is also the most hype-prone on the board: a “rogue swarm” that turns out to be crawler spam, a safety report that scores near-perfect on a narrow test but fails in real work environments, a self-replication warning that is a lab's own research with no demonstrated real-world worm. Read past both the hype and the alarm to the business signal: the honest counter-signal still stands — agents clear only a fraction of messy, high-stakes real-world tasks and the GPT-6 work-environment failures confirm it — but the direction of travel, and the money at stake, has not changed. The frontier of “what an agent can just do” keeps advancing toward the kind of multi-step work specialists bill for, even on a quiet, correction-heavy day.

IMPACT — WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE MONEY

If your revenue is “we do the work for you” — running the ads, building the funnels, managing the pipeline, producing the deliverables, writing the code — an agent that can complete a multi-step job for a fraction of a specialist's fee is direct, structural pressure on your core line, not a side risk. The value migrates from doing the task to owning the outcome: deciding what should be done, judging whether the agent did it right, and being accountable when it goes wrong. This pass hands you a sharper version of that pitch: today's news is that these systems fail in real work environments and may be genuinely hard to control — which is precisely why clients need a human who deploys agents with guardrails, review and accountability. The firms that win package the judgment and the responsibility around the agent — “we run the agents, we catch what they get wrong, we own the result” — rather than racing the agent on raw execution. The ones most exposed are those whose whole offer is labor a capable agent can now attempt.

IMPACT — WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE MONEY

If your fee is mostly execution with a thin layer of judgment on top, the agent inverts your economics — and this wave adds a second, faster-growing line of billable work: oversight and accountability. The hours you bill for building, pulling, formatting, reconciling and drafting compress first, and clients increasingly know it — which is where the "AI discount" requests come from.

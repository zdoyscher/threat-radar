IMPACT — WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE MONEY

If your fee is mostly execution with a thin layer of judgment on top, the agent inverts your economics — and this wave adds a second, faster-growing line of billable work: oversight and accountability. Two moves follow. For firms whose value really is original thinking, agents are leverage: the same three people can carry three times the clients if execution runs itself. It's real money — but it's a different business than most owners run today.

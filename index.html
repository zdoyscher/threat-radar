IMPACT — WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE MONEY

If your fee is mostly execution with a thin layer of judgment on top, the agent inverts your economics — and this wave adds a second, faster-growing line of billable work: oversight and accountability. The hours you bill for building, pulling, formatting, reconciling and drafting compress first, and clients increasingly know it — which is where the "AI discount" requests come from. Two moves follow. For firms whose value really is original thinking, agents are leverage: the same three people can carry three times the clients if execution runs itself. For firms whose value was really the doing, agents are an extinction event. "Who supervises these tools, who patches the frameworks, and who's accountable when they act" is the easiest sale you'll make all year — the headlines are writing your pitch this morning. It's real money — but it's a different business than most owners run today.
